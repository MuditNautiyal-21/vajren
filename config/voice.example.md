with. Mix registers: a recruiter, a professor, a colleague, a friend.

choice, and any amount of fine-tuning. It is the highest-leverage twenty minutes
in the whole project, and it is the one part nobody else can do for you.

every draft, and it stays local, but keep it clean anyway.
